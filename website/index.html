QUICK FAQ

Good to
know.

Got a question we missed? The game is still taking shape—check back as new builds arrive.

How do track seeds work?

A seed is a 16-character code used to generate a track. The same code recreates the same layout, scenery and pickups, so you can share a circuit and compare runs.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Racing, saved tracks and personal bests work offline. Online leaderboards and rival ghosts need a connection; finished runs can queue for upload when you’re back online.

What are the controls?

Keyboard, gamepad and touch controls are supported. Keyboard and gamepad bindings can be changed in Options, and mobile controls are designed for thumbs.

What’s available to download right now?

No public release builds have been posted yet. The platform cards above will link to official downloads as soon as they’re published—there are no unofficial mirrors here.

What does “rival ghost” mean?

A ghost is a translucent recording of a best run. Follow your own replay or share the same seeded track with up to ten of the fastest recorded opponents.
