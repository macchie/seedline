QUICK FAQ

Good to
know.

A few quick answers before your first lap.

How do track codes work?

Each 16-character code creates a unique circuit. Share it so friends can race the same corners, find their line, and compare times.

Can I play offline?

Your races, saved tracks, and personal bests are ready offline. Connect when you want to compare leaderboard times or race recorded rivals.

What are the controls?

Keyboard, gamepad and touch are all supported. Keyboard and gamepad bindings can be changed in Options, and mobile controls are designed for thumbs.

When can I play?

Launch and availability details will be shared here. Check back soon for the starting line.

What does “rival ghost” mean?

A ghost is a translucent recording of a best run. Follow your own replay or line up against the fastest recorded opponents on the same seeded track — up to ten at once.
